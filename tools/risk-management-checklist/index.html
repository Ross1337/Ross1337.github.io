The Trading Risk Management Checklist

14 rules that separate traders who survive from those who blow up. Tick them off before every session, print it, pin it next to your screen. Free, no signup — built from the same math behind our calculators.

Most accounts are not destroyed by bad analysis. They are destroyed by good analysis sized too big, a stop that got moved, a losing streak nobody planned for. Risk management is the unglamorous discipline that keeps you in the game long enough for your edge to pay out. This is the checklist we wish every trader ran before risking real money — grouped into the five decisions that actually matter. Tick each box honestly. Any one you can not check is a leak worth fixing before your next trade.
